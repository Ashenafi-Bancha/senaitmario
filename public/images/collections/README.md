# The collection photographs

The work page currently holds **three placeholder collections of two pictures
each**. Every picture in this folder today is openly-licensed stock of Ethiopian
weaving and museum costume - none of it is her work, and all of it goes as soon
as real photographs arrive.

## Where to put new ones

**Not here.** Put them in [`photos-inbox/`](../../../photos-inbox/README.md) at
the top of the project, as they come. I look at each one, group them, resize
them, name them and move them here.

If you already know which collection a picture belongs to, a subfolder per
collection saves a round trip:

```
photos-inbox/
  dinguza/
  <the next collection>/
  unsorted/
```

Any names will do - they are just for me to read.

## What a collection needs besides pictures

This is the part that blocks the page, not the photography. For each one:

| Field | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | The page prints `TODO: Collection one` today |
| **Year** | Shown beside the title |
| **City** | Where it was shown |
| **Two or three sentences** | What the collection is, in her words if possible |

Real photographs under a title reading "Collection one" look worse than the
placeholder does now, so the words matter as much as the files.

## If the collections are not known

Photographs gathered from social media often arrive without telling you which
show or season they belong to, and **a name, year and city cannot be guessed**.
Inventing a season for a real designer is the one mistake this site must not
make.

There is an honest alternative: drop the three named collections and run the
work page as a **single gallery of pieces** - each garment its own entry, with
whatever is actually known about it and nothing that is not. Named collections
can come back later, once she confirms them. Say the word and I will restructure
it; it is a change to the work page and the colour page, not to the photographs.

## Two things the colour page is waiting on

- The swatches on `/colour` are meant to be **drawn out of her real garments**.
  They stay placeholders until these photographs land.
- Each collection can own a **palette** taken from its own photography, which
  is what makes the colour field change as you move through the site. That also
  waits on the real pictures.

## Technical

- **JPG**, at least **2000px on the long edge**. Send the originals, not
  screenshots - a screenshot of a phone screen is about 1000px and shows it.
- One file per photograph. Every other size is generated automatically.
- **Tell me who took them.** Runway and editorial photography is the category
  most likely to belong to a photographer rather than to her, and it is exactly
  what this page needs. `Personal archive` is fine where it applies.

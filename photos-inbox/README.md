# Drop her photographs here

Put them in this folder exactly as they come off the phone, the camera or the
drive. Any name, any format, any size. Nothing here goes on the site until it
has been looked at and placed.

## Why this folder and not `public/images/`

Two reasons, both deliberate:

- **It is outside `public/`.** Anything under `public/` is reachable by URL the
  moment the site builds, even if no page links to it. A photograph nobody has
  cleared yet should not be one guessed address away from the open web.
- **It is out of git.** Files here are not committed, so nothing lands in the
  repository history before it has a source and a licence. Getting a file out
  of git history afterwards is genuinely hard.

## What happens next

Tell me they are in and I will, for each one:

1. Open it and see what it actually shows.
2. Put it in the right folder under `public/images/` with a proper name.
3. Resize and convert it - most originals are several megabytes and would be
   far too heavy to serve as they are.
4. Write its `alt` text, register it in `RIGHTS.md`, and wire it into the page.

## The one thing I need from you

**Who took each photograph.** Not for every file individually if that is
impractical - "these are all from her own phone" or "these are from the 2019
show, shot by X" covers a whole batch.

Copyright belongs to whoever pressed the shutter, not to the person in the
frame and not to the account that posted it. `Personal archive` is a perfectly
good answer for a family or phone photograph. If a photograph cannot be placed,
say so and it stays out rather than going up unattributed.

| --- | --- |
| Her portraits, the hero, the press shot | `public/images/portraits/` |
| Her at work, at events, day to day | `public/images/life/` |

| The six story chapters | `public/images/story/` |
| Her logo or mark | `public/images/brand/` |

You can file them yourself if you would rather - the folders are all there. But
you do not have to, and mis-filing costs more than it saves.
